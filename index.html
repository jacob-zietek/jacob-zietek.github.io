Interests

Robots will eventually replace humans in boring and dangerous tasks. This is something we should welcome with open arms, and help accelerate as much as possible.

I'm broadly passionate about using novel advancements in embodied AI to advance our lives. I enjoy working on frontier problems. Most of my work is in learning-based robotic manipulation.

I'm especially interested in companies deploying intelligent robots today. I believe we've crossed the Rubicon, end-to-end manipulation models can already provide real value, even if deployments are still limited. Deployment and fundamental research should move in tandem. Real-world deployments expose the gaps, and research pushes the frontier forward. Deployments should be a larger focus in the robotics community today.

Real-world deployments, even with simpler embodiments, will drive progress in robustness, continual learning, hardware, and supply chain onshoring far faster than purely iterating in a lab.

My other technical interests are in space and defense. I have a gut feeling I'll end up working on space robots once space is commercialized more.

I'll give out microgrants for cool projects sometimes.
